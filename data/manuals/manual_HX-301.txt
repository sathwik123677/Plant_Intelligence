EQUIPMENT MANUAL: HX-301 SHELL-AND-TUBE HEAT EXCHANGER
=======================================================
Manual ID: MAN-HX301
Equipment Tag: HX-301
Type: Shell-and-Tube Heat Exchanger
Location: Process Section

DESCRIPTION
-----------
HX-301 heats the raw feed delivered by pump P-101 using steam on the
shell side. Heated product flows out through control valve V-102 into
buffer tank T-202.

OPERATING PARAMETERS
--------------------
Feed inlet temperature: 25 C
Feed outlet temperature: 75 C
Steam pressure: 6 bar
Maximum tube-side pressure: 12 bar

MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES
----------------------
Section 3.1 - Tube Cleaning
Fouling reduces heat transfer efficiency. Clean tubes when outlet
temperature drops below 65 C at normal steam pressure. Procedure:
1. Stop feed pump P-101 and confirm standby P-102 is off.
2. Isolate steam supply.
3. Open drain valves and empty shell and tube sides.
4. Remove channel head covers.
5. Hydro-jet the tubes to remove scale and fouling.
6. Inspect tubes for corrosion or thinning.
7. Reassemble and pressure-test before returning to service.

Section 3.2 - Gasket Replacement
Replace channel head gaskets whenever the head is opened. Use gasket
set GS-301.

COMMON FAULTS
-------------
- Low outlet temperature: tube fouling or low steam pressure.
- Leakage between shell and tube side: tube failure, requires plugging.
- High pressure drop: fouling or partial blockage.
