EQUIPMENT MANUAL: T-201 STORAGE TANK
=====================================
Manual ID: MAN-T201
Equipment Tag: T-201
Type: Storage Tank, 50,000 L
Location: Storage

DESCRIPTION
-----------
T-201 stores raw feed for the process. It supplies feed pump P-101.
The tank is protected against overpressure by relief valve V-203,
which is set to lift at 10 bar.

OPERATING PARAMETERS
--------------------
Capacity: 50,000 L
Normal operating level: 40 to 80 percent
Design pressure: 12 bar
Relief valve V-203 set pressure: 10 bar

MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES
----------------------
Section 2.1 - Relief Valve V-203 Testing
Relief valve V-203 must be tested annually to confirm it lifts at the
correct set pressure. Procedure:
1. Notify control room before testing.
2. Isolate the tank if required.
3. Apply test pressure and confirm V-203 lifts at 10 bar.
4. Confirm it reseats cleanly with no leakage.
5. Record the as-found and as-left set pressures.

Section 2.2 - Level Gauge Calibration
Calibrate the level transmitter every 6 months.

COMMON FAULTS
-------------
- Relief valve V-203 leaking: worn seat, requires overhaul.
- False level readings: level transmitter drift, needs calibration.
- Feed starvation to P-101: check tank level and outlet valve.
