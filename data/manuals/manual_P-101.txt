EQUIPMENT MANUAL: P-101 CENTRIFUGAL PUMP
=========================================
Manual ID: MAN-P101
Equipment Tag: P-101
Type: Centrifugal Pump, 50 HP
Location: Feed Section

DESCRIPTION
-----------
P-101 is the primary feed pump. It draws raw feed from storage tank
T-201 through basket strainer F-501 and gate valve V-101, and
discharges into heat exchanger HX-301. Standby pump P-102 provides
backup and auto-starts if P-101 fails.

OPERATING PARAMETERS
--------------------
Normal flow rate: 120 m3/h
Normal discharge pressure: 8 bar
Maximum bearing temperature: 80 C
Normal vibration: below 4.5 mm/s

MAINTENANCE PROCEDURES
----------------------
Section 4.1 - Bearing Replacement
Bearings should be replaced when vibration exceeds 7 mm/s or every
20,000 operating hours. Procedure:
1. Isolate pump by closing valve V-101 and stopping the motor.
2. Confirm standby pump P-102 is running to maintain feed.
3. Drain the pump casing.
4. Remove coupling guard and decouple motor.
5. Remove bearing housing end cover.
6. Extract worn bearing using a bearing puller.
7. Install new bearing (part no. BR-6208) and reassemble.
8. Re-align coupling to within 0.05 mm.
9. Restart and confirm vibration is below 4.5 mm/s.

Section 4.2 - Mechanical Seal Replacement
Replace the mechanical seal if leakage exceeds 10 drops per minute.
Use seal kit SK-101. Always isolate via V-101 first.

Section 4.3 - Routine Lubrication
Grease bearings every 3 months with high-temperature grease.

COMMON FAULTS
-------------
- High vibration: usually bearing wear or coupling misalignment.
- Loss of flow: check strainer F-501 for blockage, check V-101 is open.
- Overheating: check lubrication and bearing condition.
