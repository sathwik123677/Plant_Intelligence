WORK ORDER HISTORY: HX-301 and T-201
=====================================

--- WORK ORDER WO-2023-0508 ---
Date: 2023-12-14
Equipment: HX-301
Type: Corrective Maintenance
Reported problem: Feed outlet temperature dropped to 62 C at normal
steam pressure. Tube fouling suspected.
Action taken: Stopped feed pump P-101. Isolated steam. Hydro-jetted
tubes per manual section 3.1. Replaced channel head gaskets with set
GS-301. Outlet temperature restored to 75 C.
Technician: S. Iyer
Downtime: 12 hours
